VIM COACH PLAYGROUND
====================

Do these tasks naturally. Before each task, place your cursor where the task
says and pause for about a quarter second. Pause again after completing the
task so Vim Coach can record it.


TASK 1
Place your cursor on the first X. Delete all five X letters one at a time.

keep XXXXX keep


TASK 2
Place your cursor on the h in hello. Delete only the word inside the quotes.
Delete it one letter at a time.

say "hello" now


TASK 3
Place your cursor on the a in alpha. Delete only the word inside the
parentheses, one letter at a time.

call(alpha)


TASK 4
Place your cursor on the first letter of alpha. Change alpha to beta by
deleting the old word, entering Insert mode, and typing the new word.

alpha


TASK 5
Place your cursor on the underscore. Replace it with a hyphen by deleting the
underscore, entering Insert mode, and typing the hyphen.

hello_world


TASK 6
Place your cursor on the first D. Use Visual mode to select the word DELETE,
then delete it.

DELETE this word


TASK 7
Place your cursor on the opening parenthesis. Move to the closing parenthesis
one character at a time.

(jump over this text)


TASK 8
Place your cursor on the first letter of "start". Move to the X one character
at a time.

start moving toward X please


TASK 9
Place your cursor on the O in One. Move to the T in Two one character at a
time.

One. Two.


Vim Coach sends each completed task automatically. Use :VimCoachGoals to see
the techniques it found and track practice. A technique already in your lesson
history may not appear again.
